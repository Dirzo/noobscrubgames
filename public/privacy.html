Privacy and content

Noobscrub Games is an independent studio that makes free, browser-based video games.

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies and no sign-up forms. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

What our games store

Our games may save your progress and settings in your own browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. That data stays on your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Content

  • Cartoon fantasy and sci-fi action games, suitable for general audiences.
  • No gambling, no real-money purchases, no chat with strangers and no user-uploaded content.
  • No downloads or installs. Everything runs in the browser.
